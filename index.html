Frequently Asked

Answers schools usually need.

What is Digital Solution mainly known for?

Our primary focus is Digital School ERP, school identity card solutions, admit card and result services, and practical technical assistance for school digital workflows.

Do you provide student and staff ID cards?

Yes. The portfolio is designed to showcase PVC student/staff identity cards and bulk school identity workflows.

Can a school request a Digital School ERP demo?

Yes. Schools can visit the live ERP platform or contact Digital Solution through WhatsApp for a demonstration.

Do you support government school portal work?

We provide technical and data-entry assistance for relevant government education portal workflows. Exact services depend on the portal and school requirement.

Where does Digital Solution operate?

Digital Solution is based in West Bengal and can present service coverage across North 24 Parganas, North Bengal and other supported areas.
